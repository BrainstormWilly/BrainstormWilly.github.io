### A-a-a-a-ps

<img src="/images/bleat-3-banner.jpg" width="100%"><br>*Nello and Mary, mid-bleat*

It's a slow day at the winery, so on my lunch break I decide to finish up some app testing.

Enter Nello: *"Hay"*

*"Hey"*

Nello frowns.

I look up: *"Oh, you mean h-a-y hay. You already got hay this morning"*

I return to the computer.

*"Are you selling wine?"*

I stop. Sigh. Ever since Nello has been able to equate that selling wine = hay he's become obsessed with the topic.

*"No, I'm working on my apps"*

*"Apps?"*

*"A-a-a-a-ps?"* Says Mary, who joins Nello. She's still working on her Sheeplish.

*"Yes, I'm releasing 2 apps this week and there's a lot to do"*

*"What do apps do?"*

*"A-a-a-a-ps?"*

I can now see working is not on the lunch menu.

*"Well, they hopefully help wineries sell more wine."*

*"Oh good. Are we using them?"*

*"G-o-o-o-d"*

*"Hopefully, but that's up to Alexia."*

*"Why wouldn't she. They sell wine!"*

*"W-i-i-i-ne"*

I look down at the floor, once again regretting our conversation about wine and hay.

*"I have to convince her that they'll help sell more wine first."*

*"Convinc…wait, you just said they sell wine?"*

*"W-i-i-i-ne"*

*"I said, 'hopefully'"*

*"Hope…fully?! Well, I'm hopefully going to get more hay today"*

*"H-a-a-a-y!"*

I sneer: *"Not likely!"*

*"Precisely my point."*

With that, Nello sticks his nose in the air and marches off.

Mary pauses: *"H-a-a-a-y?"*

*"Not you either, Mary...and work on your Sheeplish."*

She sulks off behind Nello: *"B-a-a-a-h!"*

I sigh. Hopefully, selling apps to wineries is a bit easier.
